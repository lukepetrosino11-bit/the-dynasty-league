League format & rosters

The Dynasty League has 12 franchises and was established in 2021 as Duck Dynasty. Players remain on their teams under fixed contracts. Sleeper hosts lineups, scoring, waivers, trades, and matchups.

Each team has 20 regular roster spots, plus 2 injured reserve spots and 2 taxi squad spots. Taxi squad players must be NFL rookies or second-year players.

The acquisition season counts as the first contract year. A Round 1 pick in 2026 expires after 2029; a player added in 2026 expires after 2027.

  • Trades preserve the existing contract.
  • A dropped player receives a new contract when acquired again.
  • All positions, including defenses, follow the same contract rules.
  • Expired players must be dropped after the fantasy season and returned to the next draft pool, subject to franchise tagging.

Each team may franchise tag one player at the end of the season to extend that player's contract by one season. Each team must choose a new player each year. The tag does not restart the original contract.

Regular season & playoffs

The regular season runs through Week 14. Each team plays its scheduled opponent and the league median each week, allowing a 2–0, 1–1, or 0–2 week, subject to ties.

Standings are determined by overall record, with points scored as the tiebreaker.

  1. The top five teams in the standings qualify for the playoffs.
  2. Of the other seven teams, the team with the most regular-season points scored receives the sixth seed, regardless of record.

The playoffs begin in Week 15. The top two seeds receive first-round byes. After the first round, the remaining teams are reseeded so the highest seed plays the lowest seed.

Draft order & Kentucky Derby lottery

The six non-playoff teams enter a lottery for draft picks 1–6, conducted during the Kentucky Derby.

Horse assignments follow the previous season's finishing order:

Previous finishAssigned horse
12th placeBest odds to win
11th placeSecond-best odds
10th placeThird-best odds
9th placeFourth-best odds
8th placeFifth-best odds
7th placeSixth-best odds

The six assigned horses determine the lottery order by their finishing positions relative to one another. The highest-finishing assigned horse earns pick 1, the next earns pick 2, and so on through pick 6. Horses outside the assigned group do not affect that relative order.

Picks 7–12 are assigned in reverse playoff finishing order: sixth place receives pick 7, fifth receives pick 8, fourth receives pick 9, third receives pick 10, the runner-up receives pick 11, and the champion receives pick 12.

Waivers, FAAB & trades
  • Each team receives an annual $100 FAAB allocation.
  • Unspent FAAB rolls over into the following season.
  • Both waiver claims and free-agent adds receive new two-season contracts.
  • The trade deadline is Week 10.
  • Draft picks may be traded.
